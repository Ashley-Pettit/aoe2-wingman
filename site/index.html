Build orders

Hera's builds, one step at a time

Fourteen builds from Hera's strategy guide, cut down to one line per step so you can read them mid game. Each step shows up, and is read out loud, as the villager it's for starts training. The Feudal click lands on your last villager.

What it does

A wingman, not an autopilot

Everything it shows you is something you could already see. It just puts it where your eyes are.

Follows your build

Reads the pop counter off your screen and moves on the moment each villager starts training.

Talks you through it

Says each step out loud, so your eyes stay on the game. Pick any voice Windows has.

Your own builds too

Write your own in Settings, or start from one of Hera's and make it yours.

Opponent insights

Their rating, how they usually open, when they hit Feudal and Castle, and how to answer it.

Knows the map

Sheep, boar and relics for the map you're on, counted off as you find them.

Nags you, nicely

Idle Town Centre, about to be housed, and a reminder to get your Feudal buildings up.

Knows when it's gone

Fall a long way behind on score and it tells you. It never resigns for you.

Keeps your record

Wins and losses by civ and map, rating game by game, and which maps to ban.

Plays well with mates

One click invites your friends to a ranked party. Go Live pauses, says sorry in chat and starts your Discord stream.

In game

Small, quiet, always on top

One fixed width, sits in a corner, locks in place with a click on the crest, and never takes focus from the game.

Install

Two minutes, start to finish

Download

Grab Wingman.exe and put it on your Desktop.

Run it

Double-click. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The exe isn't signed, that's all.

Play ranked

Pick a build from the build button and turn the speaker on. When your match starts it fills in with the map, your opponent and the first step.

Updates are one click from the bar when a new version is out. Prefer Python? The .bat version updates itself from GitHub. Full guide

Not a cheat

It only uses what you can already see: the lobby, your own screen and the public ladder. It never reads the game's memory, never shows you anything hidden, and never moves a unit. In a match the only keys it ever presses are pause and chat, and only when you press Go Live.
